Report Features

Sample Report is used by staff for clinical operations and quality control. With sample Managerial metrics attached for presentation purposes. The report contains:

Pending Response (1 of 4)

Provides Clinical Staff with Findings that have been issued by Quality Control and are pending correction. Once Corrected the Finding depopualtes.

  • Count of Findings: Displays a quick numeric value for staff to review the amount of findings they have. Vital for individual staff and main CRCs.
  • Issued by: When a finding is selected, the QC staff that issued the finding populates.

Pending Verification (2 of 4)

Shows Quality Control the Findings that have been responded to and have not been verified. This allows Quality Control to review correction made by Clinical Staff for data is accurate.

  • Center table populates findings that have been responded to, along with the number of days it took to respond to the finding.
  • Top right table populates the responses when a row is selected.

Metrics (3 of 4)

Provides a summary of staff proformance in regards to number of visits done by staff and count of Findings per visit.

  • Left table provide statistics on Clinical Staff performance.
  • The bar graph displays the number of all findings issued catergorized by Clinical Staff member.
  • Filters below allow for specific viewing for a given protocol and/or time period.
  • Right click on a given row to drill through to staff specific metrics.

Findings Directory (4 of 4)

Provides the history of all Findings to review.

  • Allows for staff to review Findings before monitor visits or audit prep.
  • Provides a wholistic story for visit findings.
