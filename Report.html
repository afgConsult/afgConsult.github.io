Report Features

Sample Report is used by staff for clinical operations and quality control. The report contains:

Pending Check In (1 of 6)

Displays subjects that have started their visit. Once the Clinical Staff checks the visit into Quality Control the visit depopulates, ensuring only visits pendings QC are shown on this page.

  • Pending Check In Count: Displays a quick numeric value for staff to review the number of pending charts. Vital for individual staff and main CRCs.
  • Shows when the Visit occurred for urgency
  • Staff filter: Allows CRCs to focus on individual tasks
  • Subject filter: Allows for a study-level filter as well for main CRCs
  • Date filter: To prioritize visits

Pending Response (2 of 6)

Provides Clinical Staff with Findings that have been issued by Quality Control and are pending correction. Once Corrected the Finding depopualtes.

  • Count of Findings: Displays a quick numeric value for staff to review the amount of findings they have. Vital for individual staff and main CRCs.
  • Issued by: When a finding is selected, the QC staff that issued the finding populates.

Pending QC (3 of 6)

Quality Control uses this page to monitor checked-in charts ready for review, enabling them to prioritize and meet review timelines while ensuring no charts are missed.

  • Color coordination populates when a visit that has been checked in is older than two days.

Pending Verification (4 of 6)

Shows Quality Control the Findings that have been responded to and have not been verified. This allows Quality Control to review correction made by Clinical Staff for data is accurate.

  • Center table populates findings that have been responded to, along with the number of days it took to respond to the finding.
  • Top right table populates the responses when a row is selected.

Findings Directory and Visit Directory (5 & 6 of 6)

Provides the history of all Visits and Findings to review.
