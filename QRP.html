Quality Report Project

Quality Data System (QDS)

Throughout my work on this project, I've focused on building a report that is central to managing the workflow between the QC staff and the clinical team. This report tracks important aspects such as Visits and Findings, which play a key role in improving operational efficiency.

The report is designed to track when charts need to be pulled into QC for review and to inform the clinical staff about which charts require their attention. It also highlights findings that are issued by QC and the responses from staff, ensuring that any issues are addressed and resolved in a timely manner.

Beyond its immediate use by the clinical and QC teams, this report serves as a crucial tool for managerial staff. It provides metrics that allow management to review performance and identify areas for improvement, enabling better decision-making based on data-driven insights.

In building this, I ensured the report is comprehensive and user-friendly, making it easier for all teams to collaborate and stay on top of their tasks. The end result is a system that streamlines the review process, improves accountability, and supports continuous improvement.
