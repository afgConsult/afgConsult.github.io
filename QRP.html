Clinical Quality Report Project

Quality Data System (QDS)

Throughout my work on this project, I've focused on building a report that is central to managing the workflow between the QC staff and the clinical team. This report tracks important aspects such as Visits and Findings, which play a key role in improving operational efficiency.

The report is designed to highlight findings that are issued by QC and the responses from staff, ensuring that any issues are addressed and resolved in a timely manner.

Beyond its immediate use by the clinical and QC teams, this report serves as a crucial tool for managerial staff. It provides metrics that allow management to review performance and identify areas for improvement, enabling better decision-making based on data-driven insights.

Report Features

The report is used by staff for clinical operations and quality control, with managerial metrics attached for presentation purposes. The report contains:

Findings Directory (1 of 3)

Tracks findings details such as the actual finding, the response, and current status.

  • Provides comprehensive tracking of all findings throughout their lifecycle
  • Displays the actual finding details for complete transparency
  • Shows responses from clinical staff to address findings
  • Tracks current status to monitor progress and resolution
  • Allows for staff to review findings before monitor visits or audit prep

Chart Directory (2 of 3)

Tracks charts checked in, when QC starts, and when QC finishes.

  • Monitors chart check-in process to ensure all required documentation is received
  • Records QC start times to track when quality control review begins
  • Documents QC completion times to measure review duration
  • Provides visibility into the quality control workflow and timing
  • Enables tracking of chart processing efficiency

Visits Summary (3 of 3)

Tracks turnaround time (TAT), staff performance, and more.

  • Measures turnaround time for various processes and workflows
  • Evaluates staff performance metrics and productivity indicators
  • Provides comprehensive visit statistics and analytics
  • Tracks key performance indicators for operational efficiency
  • Enables management oversight of clinical operations
